No. We manage and optimize the acquisition system, but results depend on factors including the offer, market, budget, audience, and sales execution.

Yes. Paid acquisition is a core component of the demand generation engine.

Yes. We transform existing founder expertise and long-form insights into short-form videos, carousels, and advertising creative.
Do you build the funnel?
Yes. We build the landing pages, lead magnets, qualification forms, tracking architecture, and conversion infrastructure.

Yes. We connect the lead-generation system directly into your existing CRM infrastructure where technically feasible.

No. Revenue depends on your complete sales execution — including your offer economics, sales team velocity, follow-up cadence, and closing ability.

No. Advertising media spend is paid directly to advertising platforms and is separate from the One Life Media management fee.
