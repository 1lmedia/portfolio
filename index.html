The Problem

THE PROBLEM MOST FOUNDERS
ARE STUCK WITH

You know a lot. You have expertise, opinions, experience, and results. But almost no one outside your immediate circle knows it.

And it's not because you don't want to communicate. It's because the model you've been sold doesn't work when you actually have a company to run.

❌
Becoming A Content Creator

You try. You post for a few days. Stop. Try again. Stop again. Because you can't shut down the operation every week to brainstorm topics, record and edit content. You're a business owner, not an influencer. That constant tension between what you should be posting and what you actually need to do inside the business brings everything to a halt.

❌
Hiring A Social Media Manager

They post. They generate views. But they don't generate customers. Because generic content without your thesis, your market perspective, and your voice is just noise. And noise doesn't close high-ticket deals.

❌
Depending On Outbound

It works until it doesn't. And when it stops working, you realize you haven't built anything beyond a cold list of people who don't know you, don't trust you, and resist whatever price you put in front of them.

❌
Depending On Your Network

Referrals are great. But you don't control when they come. And while you wait, the competitor who built a qualified audience is getting warm leads every day, closing with less friction, and charging more.

The problem isn't your expertise.
The problem is that you don't have distribution.

And without distribution, what you know stays trapped — in your head, with your existing clients, and inside your immediate network.

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

I can build this operation internally. Wouldn't that be cheaper than hiring you?
Cheap and expensive depend on what you consider cheap and expensive. If this type of strategy were already part of your DNA, you would have implemented it already. You haven't — because this isn't what you live and breathe. It looks simple from the outside. In practice, it's complex. You see influencers with massive reach who generate no revenue, and business owners who know how to sell but can't identify the bottlenecks in their distribution. I've had companies pay me, receive the exact playbook for how we operate — and still fail to execute it. Not because they didn't work hard. Because this needs to be part of the operator's DNA. We live and breathe this 24/7.
How do I know these results weren't a one-off?
I'm not going to pretend everyone will get the same numbers. Every operation has its own variables. What we do is isolate the factors that work and amplify what can be replicated. What I can tell you is this: I didn't invent this. What I apply is what Gary Vaynerchuk has been doing for more than 20 years. What's rare is someone executing it with a focus on commercial results, not vanity metrics.
My niche is different. Will this work in my market?
If your market depends on trust to close deals — yes. That's exactly where massive personal brand distribution reduces friction, shortens the sales cycle, and lowers CAC. The only scenario where this may not be ideal is if you sell low-ticket products or operate entirely in B2C. My specialty is scaling personal brands for B2B.
I've tried creating content before and it didn't work. Why would this be different?
Because now you have someone who has executed this, identified the bottlenecks, and built the frameworks to guide you. The difference is that you won't be navigating in the dark, trying to figure out what to fix. You execute. We lead.
Won't 3,000 videos in 90 days look like low-quality mass-produced content?
The only bad thing about 3,000 videos in 90 days is that they're not 30,000. Quality is subjective. What isn't subjective is reach and retention — and that's what we optimize for. Bad content, to us, is content that doesn't generate retention or reach. Everything else is an opinion.
What if I record the content and the operation doesn't generate the expected results?
What I guarantee unconditionally is the delivery: 3,000 pieces of content distributed over 90 days. If we go beyond 90 days, we keep publishing until we hit the number. Simple as that. Our business depends on your results — that's why we treat every operation as if it were our own company.
Do I need to be on camera a lot? I'm not comfortable in front of the camera.
You need to record the 10–12 hours of long-form content. We'll help you make that process as natural as possible. Once that's done, the operation runs without requiring you to show up every day. But if the idea of being on camera bothers you so much that you absolutely refuse to do it — then this isn't for you. And while you're deciding, your competitor is building authority in the market that could be yours. It's a choice.
How is this different from hiring a regular social media agency?
A traditional agency delivers a content calendar and social presence. Our focus is commercial results — more reach among your ideal customers, designed to build trust, shorten the sales cycle, and attract new customers without relying entirely on outbound. These are completely different propositions.
I already have an internal marketing team. How does this fit in?
Even better. Your team continues handling the other parts of your marketing operation while we take ownership of personal brand distribution. In practice, we become an additional arm of your team, focused specifically on this channel.
Why now? My business already sells well through outbound and referrals.
That's exactly why. When the business is already healthy, you have the margin to deploy this strategy without the pressure of needing an immediate return. Content is exponential — but it takes time to gain momentum. Those who start when they're doing well harvest when they're doing even better. The best time to plant was yesterday.
