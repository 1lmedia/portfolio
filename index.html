The Problem

YOUR PROBLEM
ISN'T CONTENT.
IT'S
DISTRIBUTION.

You already have expertise. You probably already record podcasts, interviews, webinars, sales calls, YouTube videos or other long-form content.

The problem? Most of it gets published once and disappears. A single recording can contain dozens of valuable ideas, insights and moments.

But nobody has the time to watch hours of footage, find the best moments, write hooks, edit every clip, add captions or manage five different platforms. So your best ideas stay buried inside your long-form content.

Ideas Stay Buried

Hours of raw footage hide dozens of moments worth publishing — and nobody on your team has time to find them.

One Post, Then Gone

Content gets published once and disappears — instead of compounding into daily distribution across every channel.

Five Platforms, No Bandwidth

Editing, captioning, formatting and publishing across five channels every day is a full-time job on its own.

The One Life Media Method

WE DON'T
JUST CLIP
YOUR VIDEOS.
WE PACKAGE IDEAS.

Randomly cutting 30 seconds from a podcast isn't a content strategy. We look at the idea behind the clip and structure it for short-form consumption.

Every clip is built around a strong opening, clear messaging, strategic cuts, captions, visual emphasis, branding and a CTA opportunity — not just a shorter version of the original.

The goal isn't to produce more random videos. The goal is to extract more business value from the content you're already creating.
